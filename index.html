Rock Paper Fists · 猜拳格斗

Privacy Policy

In short: this game collects no data about you.

Effective October 19, 2026

Nothing is collected

No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics. The game never sends anything about you to the developer or anyone else.

Kept on this phone

Settings, record, replays, skins, tutorial progress and the move habits the computer opponent learns stay on this phone and are deleted with the game. Your phone’s own backup may include them.

Playing face to face

Two phones on one Wi-Fi or hotspot connect directly, not over the internet, and only swap what the match needs: moves, characters, skins, version. An Android host uses its phone model as the room name.

Replay codes

A replay code leaves the game only when you copy or send it yourself. The game reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste.

Permissions

Network and Wi-Fi only, to play together and find rooms. No location, camera, microphone or contacts, and no access to your files or photos.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the game and the web page are updated together and the date above changes.

Contact

Questions? Email info@maxtt.com.

隐私政策

一句话：这个游戏不收集你的任何数据。

2026 年 10 月 19 日起生效

什么都不收集

没有账号、没有服务器、没有广告、没有统计。游戏不会把你的任何信息发给开发者或别人。

只存在这台手机上

设置、战绩、回放、皮肤、教学进度，还有电脑对手记下的出招习惯，只存在这台手机上，删掉游戏就一起删掉。手机自己的备份可能包含它们。

面对面联机

两台手机在同一个 Wi-Fi 或热点里直接相连，不经过互联网，只传对战要用的：出招、角色、皮肤、版本。建房的安卓手机用手机型号当房间名，同一网络里的手机看得到。

回放码

只有你自己复制或发给别人，回放码才会离开游戏；只有你点「粘贴」，游戏才读剪贴板。

用到的权限

只用网络和 Wi-Fi（联机、找房间）。不用位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录，也不读你的文件和照片。

儿童

游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

改动

这份说明有改动时，游戏里和网页上会一起更新，并改上面的日期。

联系

有问题请写信到 info@maxtt.com。
